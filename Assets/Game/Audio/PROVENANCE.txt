Original procedural prototype sound effects for this project.
Created from mathematical oscillators, filtered pseudorandom noise and envelopes
using Python's standard library. No third-party recordings or sample packs.
These are synthesized approximations, not recordings of an actual KAMAZ.

9 mono PCM WAV files, 32000 Hz / 16 bit:
DieselIdle, DieselLoad, TireRoad, TireSlip, ChainRattle,
AirBrakeRelease, GearShift, ChainFitting, TruckHorn.

Runtime clips/volumes/response/distances are configured in KamazAudio.asset.
ConfigureTruckAudio.cs imports clips and wires the scene's audio sources.
ValidateTruckAudio.cs checks PCM, playback, output samples and response/events.
Generated source script: work/generate_truck_audio.py (outside Assets).
