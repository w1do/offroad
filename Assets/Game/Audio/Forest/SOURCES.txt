Changes: first approximately 27 seconds, downmix to mono, box-filter downsample
to 24 kHz PCM16, conservative peak normalization, one-second loop crossfade;
resulting loops approximately 26 seconds. Unity imports as Vorbis in memory.
These are genuine field recordings, not synthesized replacement effects.
The source describes a public park in Adelaide, South Australia, in winter.
They provide prototype ambience; no claim is made that the species are native
to Siberian taiga. Truck sounds remain separate from this recorded ambience.
